Electromechanical Works Contracting

  • Pump room installation, modification and equipment replacement.
  • Supply, installation and replacement of water pumps and associated assemblies.
  • Pump manifold, suction and discharge connection works.
  • Installation and modification of mechanical and electrical pump-control systems.

  • Electromechanical modifications for schools, accommodation buildings and industrial facilities.
  • Site installation and equipment connection works.

Electromechanical Equipment Installation and Maintenance

  • Preventive and corrective maintenance of water pumps.
  • Booster, roof, filling, irrigation and submersible pump servicing.
  • Swimming-pool filter pump repair and maintenance.
  • Sewage and STP pumping equipment servicing.
  • Pump and motor overhauling, rewinding and component replacement.
  • Mechanical seal, bearing and impeller replacement.
  • Pressure-switch, flow-sensor and pump-control troubleshooting.
  • Maintenance of associated motors, fans, blowers and selected industrial equipment.
  • Installation and maintenance of selected water heaters, pool heaters and HVAC equipment.

Electrical Fittings/Fixtures Repairing and Maintenance

  • Electrical panel component replacement, including breakers, contactors and relays.
  • Distribution board and motor-control equipment maintenance.
  • Electrical cable pulling, laying, termination and replacement.
